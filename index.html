Research

Research Areas

We focus on infrastructure and methods for evaluating robotic systems before deploying them to real-world hardware.

01

Robot Simulation

Physics-based robot and environment simulation using modern robotics simulation platforms.

02

Policy Evaluation

Automated testing of robotic policies across controlled, reproducible scenarios.

03

Failure Analysis

Identifying why robotic systems fail and generating targeted scenarios to expose weaknesses.

04

Sim-to-Real

Measuring and reducing differences between simulated behavior and real robot performance.

05

Robot Manipulation

Evaluation and experimentation for grasping, manipulation, control and interaction tasks.

06

Benchmarking

Reproducible experiments, metrics, automated testing and structured benchmark reporting.
