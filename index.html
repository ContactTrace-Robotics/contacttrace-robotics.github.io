ContactTrace Robotics

Independent Robotics Research Platform

ContactTrace Robotics focuses on robot simulation, benchmarking, policy evaluation, and sim-to-real validation.

Research Areas

  • Robot Simulation
  • Simulation Benchmarking
  • Robot Policy Evaluation
  • Failure Analysis
  • Sim-to-Real Validation
  • Robot Manipulation

Projects

Robot Evaluation Platform

Automated evaluation infrastructure for testing robot policies, identifying failure modes, and generating structured benchmark results.

Real2Isaac

Pipeline for transforming real-world captured environments into simulation-ready scenes for NVIDIA Isaac Sim.

Failure-Aware Robotics Evaluation

Research on identifying robot-policy failures and generating targeted scenarios for evaluation and improvement.

Collaborate

We welcome collaboration with students, researchers, and engineers interested in robotics simulation, manipulation, benchmarking, and sim-to-real research.
